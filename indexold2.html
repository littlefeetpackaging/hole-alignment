Bounds

Below the upper bound the holes count as apart. At or above completion they count as aligned. The offset each bound means is shown in mm.

How the reading is worked out

Each frame, the pixels inside the circle are split into "opening" and "metal" with an automatic (Otsu) threshold; the last good threshold is kept when the circle is uniform, so fully apart and fully aligned both read correctly. The open fraction is compared with the most that can ever show through (the smaller hole's area), giving the overlap %. The overlap of two circles of the diameters you entered is then solved backwards for the distance between their centres, so a crescent of a given size becomes an offset in mm. If the holes differ in size, any offset up to half the difference still reads fully open: that is the float shown under Holes. The centre of the tinted region shows which side the lower hole sits on.
